How It Works

Governed verification in three steps

01

Define Pipeline Spec

Write a Glyphser specification for your ML pipeline. Define inputs, transforms, and expected outputs in a declarative YAML config.

02

Run & Verify

Execute your pipeline through the Glyphser runtime. Every run is recorded with deterministic replay guarantees and full artifact hashing.

03

Review Evidence

Access conformance reports, lineage traces, and pass/fail gates. Every verification produces audit-ready evidence bundles.
